\section{Clifford Inner Product}

The inner product for vectors is defined as
\begin{align}
\left\langle a\left\vert b\right.  \right\rangle  & =\left\langle
ab\right\rangle _{0}=\left\langle \sum_{j,k}a_{j}b_{k}x_{j}x_{k}\right\rangle
_{0}=\left\langle \sum_{j}a_{j}b_{j}x_{j}^{2}+\sum_{j\neq k}a_{j}b_{k}%
x_{j}x_{k}\right\rangle _{0}\\
& =\left\langle \sum_{j}a_{j}b_{j}x_{j}^{2}\right\rangle _{0}=\left\langle
\sum_{j}a_{j}b_{j}I\right\rangle _{0}=\sum_{j}a_{j}b_{j}tr\left\{  I\right\}
=\sum_{j}a_{j}b_{j}%
\end{align}

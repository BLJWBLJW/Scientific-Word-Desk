Getting that light, crispy, and bubbly batter on fish can be tricky in an air fryer. Here are some reasons why your batter may be falling flat and how to fix it:

Type of Batter: Air fryers work by circulating hot air, which isn't ideal for liquid batters. Liquid batters tend to drip off or solidify unevenly before they can crisp up.

Fix: Try using a dry coating like seasoned flour, breadcrumbs, panko, or cornmeal instead. If you want a batter-like texture, dip the fish in egg or buttermilk first, then coat it generously in your dry mixture.

Moisture on the Fish: If the fish is too wet, the batter won’t stick properly.

Fix: Pat the fish completely dry with paper towels before coating it.

Preheating the Air Fryer: If the air fryer isn’t preheated, the coating won't crisp up quickly.

Fix: Preheat your air fryer for 3–5 minutes to the required temperature before placing the fish inside.

Layering and Airflow: Crowding the basket can cause uneven cooking, and the batter might not crisp up.

Fix: Arrange the fish pieces in a single layer with space around them for proper airflow.

Spray with Oil: Air fryers rely on a little oil to get that golden, crispy crust.

Fix: Lightly spray the coated fish with cooking oil or use an oil brush to ensure even crisping.

\end{array}
\right)
\end{equation}


The vectors $\left(
\begin{array}
[c]{cc}%
\mathbf{w} & \overline{\mathbf{w}}%
\end{array}
\right)  $ are are defined modulo $SU(1,1)$ rotations. 

A geminal determines an antisymmetric matrix wrt a conb basis. This matrix
determines an antisymmetric operator.  The geminal is invariant to $SU\left(
2\right)  ^{s}$ in the non degenerate case, while the operator is invariant to
$SU\left(  1,1\right)  ^{s}.$

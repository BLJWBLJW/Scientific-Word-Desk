In a locally convex topological vector space, a compact convex set is the closed convex hull of its extreme points.

For finite-dimensional spaces, this simplifies to: a compact convex set is the convex hull of its extreme points.

\subsection*{Finite-Dimensional Case:}

In \(\mathbb{R}^n\), a compact convex set \(C\) is the convex hull of its extreme points. This means every point in \(C\) can be expressed as a convex combination of its extreme points.

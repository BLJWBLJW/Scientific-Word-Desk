\section{Exponentials}

The exterior exponential $\operatorname{Exp}\left\{  A\right\}  $ is defined
as
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Exp}\left\{  A\right\}  =I+A+\frac{1}{2!}A\wedge A+\frac{1}%
{3!}A\wedge A\wedge A+\cdots,
\end{equation}
while the usual exponential as
\begin{equation}
\exp\left\{  A\right\}  =I+A+\frac{1}{2!}A^{2}+\frac{1}{3!}A^{3}+\cdots.
\end{equation}
The usual exponential has the conventional factorization property
\begin{equation}
\exp\left\{  A_{1}+A_{2}\right\}  =\,\exp\left\{  A_{1}\right\}  \exp\left\{
A_{2}\right\}
\end{equation}
when
\begin{equation}
\left[  A_{1},A_{2}\right]  =0.
\end{equation}
We concentrate on $A\in\mathcal{F}_{2},$ (geminal operators), as we are
interested in the Gauge Invariant subalgebra $\mathcal{F}_{e}.$
